About

I develop enterprise web applications with Angular, React, Next.js and TypeScript. In six years at Mobiliva R&D Center, I built the interfaces of products such as a bulk SMS platform, a consent management system, a gift voucher manager and a digital catalog.

My strongest areas are setting up real-time data flows with WebSocket, SignalR and RabbitMQ, and building interfaces with Tailwind CSS and DevExtreme that work well on every screen size. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Okan University.
